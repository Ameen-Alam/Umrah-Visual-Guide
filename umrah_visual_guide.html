لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ عُمْرَةً

Umrah, step by step

Six stations, one fixed order. What you do, what you say, and what to avoid at each one.

Must do Sunnah (recommended) Any time / your choice

Tap a station to jump to it. The order never changes.

1Ihram must

At the Miqat — before the boundary, never after it.

Ihram is not the white cloth. It is the intention to begin Umrah, said at the Miqat. The cloth is only how a man dresses once he is in that state.

Before the intention sunnah

  1. Trim nails, remove body hair, tidy moustache.
  2. Full bath (ghusl) — women too, even during menses.
  3. Men: perfume on the body and beard, not on the cloth.
  4. Men: wear the two cloths. Women: normal loose, modest clothes in any colour.
  5. If a prayer is due, pray it. There is no special "ihram prayer".
Say this — the intention
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ عُمْرَةً

Labbayk Allāhumma ʿUmrah

Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah.

Afraid of not being able to finish (illness, pregnancy)? Add: Allāhumma maḥillī ḥaythu ḥabastanī — "My exit from ihram is wherever You stop me." Then no penalty if you must stop.
Then keep repeating — the Talbiyah
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Labbayk Allāhumma labbayk, labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk, innal-ḥamda wan-niʿmata laka wal-mulk, lā sharīka lak

Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise, favour and sovereignty are Yours. You have no partner.

Men aloud, women quietly. Each person on their own, not as a chorus. Stop when you start Tawaf.

Now in ihram — until your hair is cut

Not allowed
  • Cutting hair or nails
  • Perfume, scented soap, scented wipes
  • Intimacy, flirting, marriage contract
  • Hunting
  • Arguing, insulting, fighting
  • Men: stitched clothes, head cover
  • Women: niqab, gloves
Allowed — don't worry
  • Shower, unscented soap, change the cloth
  • Watch, glasses, ring, belt, bandage
  • Umbrella, shade, air-conditioning
  • Scratching, combing gently
  • Eating any halal food
  • Blanket at night (men: not over the head)
  • Phone, talking, resting
Beginner mistakes here
  • Crossing the Miqat and planning to "do ihram at the hotel".
  • Perfuming the cloth instead of the body.
  • Men wearing underwear or socks under the cloths.
  • Stopping the Talbiyah on reaching Makkah — it continues until Tawaf begins.

Broke a rule by forgetting? No penalty; stop when you remember. Deliberately? Fast 3 days, or feed 6 poor people, or sacrifice a sheep — any one.

2Enter the Haram sunnah

Masjid al-Haram, Makkah. Any gate is fine.

  1. Make wudu first — you need it for Tawaf.
  2. Shoes go in a bag you carry. Never leave them at the door.
  3. Note your gate name and number. You'll need it to get out.
  4. Enter right foot first and say the mosque dua.
  5. Walk straight to the Kaaba. No "greeting prayer" here — Tawaf is the greeting.
Say this — entering
بِسْمِ اللهِ، وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ

Bismillāh, waṣ-ṣalātu was-salāmu ʿalā rasūlillāh, Allāhumma-ftaḥ lī abwāba raḥmatik

In the name of Allah, blessings and peace on the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.

First sight of the Kaaba your choice

There is no fixed dua from the Prophet ﷺ for this moment. Raise your hands, say Allāhu Akbar, and ask for whatever you want — it is a moment when dua is answered. Then stop the Talbiyah and go to the Black Stone corner.

3Tawaf must

Around the Kaaba. Seven circuits, anti-clockwise, Kaaba on your left.

Before you start

  • Wudu. Required. Women in menses wait until pure (see below).
  • Men: bare the right shoulder (cloth under the right arm, over the left) — for this Tawaf only.
  • Stand in line with the Black Stone, using the green light on the far wall.
Say this — at the Black Stone, start of every circuit
بِسْمِ اللهِ، اللهُ أَكْبَرُ

Bismillāh, Allāhu Akbar

Kiss it only if it's easy. Otherwise touch it and kiss your hand. Otherwise — the normal case — just face it and point with your right palm. No kissing the hand after pointing.

Say this — between the Yemeni corner and the Black Stone, every circuit
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Rabbanā ātinā fid-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fil-ākhirati ḥasanatan wa qinā ʿadhāban-nār

Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

The rest of each circuit your choice

There is no special dua for circuit one, two, or three. Booklets that give a different dua per circuit are made up. Use the time for your own dua in your own language, Qur'an, or dhikr — quietly, while walking.

Do
  • Men: walk briskly with short steps for circuits 1–3, normally for 4–7. Skip it if crowded.
  • Keep moving at the crowd's pace.
  • Count on your fingers or a counter.
  • If prayer starts: pray, then continue from where you stopped.
  • Unsure if it was 5 or 6? Call it 5.
Don't
  • Cut through the Hijr — that circuit doesn't count.
  • Push or fight to reach the Black Stone.
  • Kiss or point at the Yemeni corner or the other corners.
  • Rub the Kaaba's cloth for blessing.
  • Stop in the flow to pray or take photos.
  • Women: no brisk walking (raml).

Tap when you cross the Black Stone line

Women and monthly bleeding

Enter ihram normally and do everything except Tawaf. Wait until pure, bathe, then Tawaf → Sa'i → hair. Ihram rules stay on until then. If your period begins after Tawaf, carry on with Sa'i — purity isn't needed for Sa'i.

Beginner mistakes here
  • Reading a different booklet dua for each circuit, or following a leader shouting duas.
  • Starting the count somewhere other than the Black Stone line.
  • Walking around with the right shoulder bare for days afterwards.
  • Restarting the whole Tawaf after a prayer break — just resume.

4Maqam Ibrahim & Zamzam sunnah

Right after the seventh circuit.

  1. Men: cover both shoulders again.
  2. Walk to Maqam Ibrahim (the golden enclosure facing the Kaaba door) and recite the verse below.
  3. Pray two short rak'ahs — al-Kafirun in the first, al-Ikhlas in the second. If the Maqam area is crowded, pray them anywhere in the mosque. Same reward.
  4. Drink Zamzam from any dispenser. Say Bismillāh, face the Kaaba if you can, drink your fill, make dua.
  5. If easy, go back and point to the Black Stone once more. Then head to Safa.
Say this — arriving at the Maqam
وَاتَّخِذُوا مِن مَّقَامِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ مُصَلًّى

Wattakhidhū min maqāmi Ibrāhīma muṣallā

And take the station of Ibrahim as a place of prayer. (2:125)

Dua with Zamzam your choice
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا وَاسِعًا، وَشِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ

Allāhumma innī as'aluka ʿilman nāfiʿan, wa rizqan wāsiʿan, wa shifā'an min kulli dā'

O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, wide provision, and cure from every illness. — Zamzam is for whatever you drink it for; any dua works.

Beginner mistakes here
  • Pushing into the Tawaf flow to pray exactly behind the Maqam.
  • Praying long rak'ahs there while hundreds wait.
  • Kissing or rubbing the Maqam's glass — it has no such virtue.
  • Walking in front of people praying.

5Sa'i must

Between Safa and Marwah. Seven laps, start at Safa, finish at Marwah.

Say this — once only, as you approach Safa the first time
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِن شَعَائِرِ اللهِ، فَمَنْ حَجَّ الْبَيْتَ أَوِ اعْتَمَرَ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْهِ أَن يَطَّوَّفَ بِهِمَا، وَمَن تَطَوَّعَ خَيْرًا فَإِنَّ اللهَ شَاكِرٌ عَلِيمٌ

Innaṣ-Ṣafā wal-Marwata min shaʿā'irillāh… (2:158)

أَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ اللهُ بِهِ

Abda'u bimā bada'Allāhu bih

I begin with what Allah began with. — Not repeated on later laps.

On Safa, and again on Marwah

  1. Climb a little, face the Kaaba.
  2. Raise your hands as in dua (palms up, not like the takbir of prayer).
  3. Say Allāhu Akbar three times, then the dhikr below.
  4. Make your own dua — long as you like.
  5. Repeat dhikr and dua so the dhikr is said three times in all.
Say this — on Safa and on Marwah
لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ

Lā ilāha illAllāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamdu wa huwa ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīr. Lā ilāha illAllāhu waḥdah, anjaza waʿdah, wa naṣara ʿabdah, wa hazamal-aḥzāba waḥdah

There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and the praise, and He has power over all things. There is no god but Allah alone; He kept His promise, gave victory to His servant, and defeated the confederates alone.

Crowded? Shorten it. This part is Sunnah; the walking is what's required.
While walking your choice
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْأَعَزُّ الْأَكْرَمُ

Rabbi-ghfir war-ḥam, innaka antal-aʿazzul-akram

My Lord, forgive and have mercy; You are the Mightiest, the Most Generous. — Or any dua, Qur'an, or dhikr.

Beginner mistakes here
  • Starting from Marwah.
  • Counting there-and-back as one lap and doing fourteen.
  • Reciting the Safa verse on every lap.
  • Women running in the green zone; able men strolling through it.
  • Redoing Sa'i because wudu broke — it isn't needed.

6Cut the hair must

Barbers are just outside the Marwah exit. Women: in private.

The moment the hair is cut, ihram ends. Perfume, normal clothes, nails — everything is allowed again. Your Umrah is complete.

Beginner mistakes here
  • Changing into normal clothes in the hotel before the haircut "to get comfortable".
  • Snipping three hairs and calling it done.
  • Women cutting in public, or from the front only.
  • Trimming the beard instead of the head.

After Umrah no fixed order

None of this is part of Umrah. Do what you can, in any order.

  • Extra Tawaf — any time, as often as you like; it's the special worship of Makkah. Two rak'ahs after each.
  • Every prayer in the Haram — arrive early; it fills up long before the adhan.
  • Sit and look at the Kaaba, read Qur'an, make dua. Late night is quiet.
  • Repeating Umrah from Tan'eem — scholars differ; many say extra Tawaf is better. Don't turn it into a number race.
  • Madinah — pray in the Prophet's Mosque, greet him ﷺ with As-salāmu ʿalayka yā Rasūlallāh, then Abu Bakr and Umar. Make your dua facing the Qibla, not the grave. Book the Rawdah early. Pray two rak'ahs at Quba with wudu made at your hotel.

عُمْرَةً مَقْبُولَةً وَذَنْبًا مَغْفُورًا

May your Umrah be accepted and your sins forgiven. Where scholars differ on a detail, this guide follows the most widely taught view — ask a scholar you trust for your own situation.
